# Surface brief: Elanth landing (src/pages/index.astro)

Scope: single landing page, neutral Spanish, no stated location. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: owners/managers of traditional businesses with ERP + warehouse, little digital presence.
Action: "Obtener asesoría y demo gratuita" -> simple contact form (nombre, empresa, contacto); business details clearly OPTIONAL.
Proof: no clients/metrics/testimonials exist; demonstration orders are synthetic and labeled as such.
Constraints: Astro + Tailwind; desktop and mobile; future departments shown as roadmap only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Tú solo recibes los pedidos." The page is the order printer (comandera) where orders from every channel land as thermal tickets, already synced to one inventory. Refuses the navy SaaS hero + dashboard screenshot + three icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Navy #0B1F33 machine/warehouse ground owning most of the surface; thermal paper #F4F7FA tickets with torn edges, dashed tear lines, barcodes, tabular time stamps; slate #263445 device bodies; brand blue #2590BE for channel ink; teal #19C3B1 is the ONE lit element per screen (CTA, printer LED); amber #F5A623 for pending/roadmap. Wide heavy grotesque (Archivo expanded) echoing the wordmark; condensed mono only for ticket data. Sync state drawn by glyph + stroke, never color alone.

STORY: Visitor sees orders from Mercado Libre, Amazon, eBay, Marketplace printing into one place, understands Elanth builds the store, syncs ERP and channels, runs it with its own team and ads; believes they do less, not more; asks for the free asesoría + demo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: headline in full-measure bands "Tú recibes los pedidos. Nosotros, todo lo demás.", one-line value prop, teal CTA + secondary "Ver cómo funciona". Right 5/12: printer slot device with LED, tickets emerging one by one (channel, #, hour, SKU lines, barcode), a synced stock rail under it decrementing on every channel at once. Mobile: headline, CTA, then printer at full width.

FORM: Thermal order ticket / comandera; position 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 50459093. Code-led. Signature interaction: each printed ticket decrements the same SKU stock on every channel simultaneously; hovering a service lights the part of the flow it governs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
