# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Tailwind CSS (pinned by the user). Static landing page; must work well on desktop and mobile.

## Users

Owners and managers of traditional Venezuelan businesses (distributors, wholesalers, retailers, manufacturers) that sell physically and have little or no digital presence. They already run an administrative system (ERP) and a warehouse, and want to sell online without hiring a tech team or multiplying operational workload. Many sell B2B to other companies.

## Product Purpose

Elanth is a B2B technology solutions agency focused on human capital and tangible commercial results. It is the bridge between traditional companies and the digital world, building software ecosystems that fit each client's business model exactly. Current phase: **E-Commerce 360°**, a turnkey ("llave en mano") transition to e-commerce. Success = the client scales revenue and web presence without growing its operational load.

## Positioning

Not just a store: Elanth centralizes and automates every sales channel and, uniquely, provides Elanth-trained staff who run the client's digital store. The client only receives orders and manages its physical warehouse.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current services (E-Commerce 360°):
- **Desarrollo y Digitalización**: custom e-commerce platforms for companies with no digital life, adapting the physical model to the web.
- **Omnicanalidad y Sincronización**: integration between the new store and the client's existing ERP; centralized inventory to sell simultaneously on Mercado Libre, Amazon, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, etc. from one control panel.
- **E-Commerce B2B Avanzado**: specific business rules, stock control, dynamic price lists; exclusive prices and conditions per corporate client, automated.
- **Gestión Operativa Delegada (Auto-gestión)**: Elanth-trained personnel administer the client's digital store.
- **Marketing y Tráfico Digital**: social media management, content creation, hyper-personalized ad campaigns per niche.

Future vision (not yet available; must be presented as roadmap, never as current offer): Elanth Code (advanced software, integrations, custom apps), Elanth Media (content, design, branding, visual communication), Elanth Games (interactive experiences, videogames), Elanth Industry (software and advanced solutions for industry).

Primary conversion: **"Obtener asesoría y demo gratuita"** — a simple contact form (name, company, contact) so Elanth can reach out; an extra field about the business is explicitly **optional** and must read as optional. No backend yet: the form must be ready to wire to a form service.

## Product Principles

1. Results over technology talk: every service is framed by its commercial outcome.
2. Turnkey means the client does less, not more: show the work Elanth absorbs.
3. One panel, many channels: centralization is the core mechanism.
4. Honest about phase: current offer is E-Commerce 360°; the hub is a vision.
